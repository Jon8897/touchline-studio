How it works

Three steps, one evening a week

  1. Pick the plan

    Choose your formation and the patterns you want to work on: how you build up, how you press, how you attack the box.

  2. Build the session

    Use a template or let the planner build one for your squad size and the day of the week. Swap drills, change timings, add your own.

  3. Send the link

    Publish the week. Share one link in your team group chat. Players see the match, the training and the animations on their phone.

Questions

FAQ

Do my players need to download anything?

No. They open the link you send in your team chat. It works on any phone, with no app and no account.

Does it work on my phone at training?

Yes. It’s built mobile-first, including the session timer that runs your whole session on the pitch.

Is it safe for youth teams?

Yes. Players don’t create accounts, there’s no messaging and no photos, and every player page has a “Report a concern” link. Coaches agree to post first names only. See how we keep players safe on our Safeguarding page.

What age groups is it for?

Any age. The drills scale from small-sided games to 11 v 11, and the planner only picks drills that suit the number of players you have.

Can my assistant coach help plan?

Yes. Invite them to your team and they can build and publish the weekly plan with you.

Is my data safe?

Your account is password-protected, everything travels encrypted, and your plans are stored on our own secure server. We don’t sell data, show adverts or track anyone. Players never need an account, and player pages show only what you choose to post. Read our Privacy policy and Safeguarding page.

What does it cost?

There’s a free plan for one team. The Coach plan is £4.99 a month or £39 a year, with a 14-day free trial, and the Club plan is £19.99 a month for up to 12 teams. Founding coaches from the beta get half price for life.

Can I cancel?

Yes, any time, from Team week → Plan & billing. You keep access until the end of the period you’ve paid for, and nothing is deleted if you go back to the free plan. Cancel during the free trial and you won’t be charged at all. See our refund policy.
