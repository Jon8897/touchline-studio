Write your plan, the board builds it

Name your formation and theirs (e.g. “we play 4-3-3 against their 4-4-2”), then describe each phase in order. Recognised: build-up, when they press us, go long, switch via the keeper, overlap / cross / cut-back, counter-attack, press high / man-to-man, pressing trap, back-pass trigger, mid block, low block, defending crosses, counter-press, rest defence.
