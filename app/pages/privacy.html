Players and children

Players don't create accounts. The only player information we hold is what a coach adds for team sheets: a first name or nickname and a shirt number. The team sheet on a player page shows only the players picked to start or be a substitute; who isn't available is visible to coaches only, and no reason is ever recorded. A player page shows only what the coach chooses to post, and opening it doesn't set any cookie or record who you are. Because players may be under 18, our Terms and Safeguarding page tell coaches not to post players' personal details such as surnames, phone numbers, addresses, dates of birth, medical information or photos. If you see something on a player page that shouldn't be there, use Report a concern at the bottom of the page and we'll remove it.

Coach accounts are for adults (18 and over) only.

How long we keep it

  • Your account and everything you created: until you delete your account. You can do that yourself at any time.
  • Backups: kept for 14 days, then deleted automatically, so deleted data disappears from backups within 14 days.
  • Published player pages: until the coach deletes them, or the account is deleted.
  • Squad lists: until the coach removes the player, deletes the team or deletes the account.
  • Feedback, reports and requests: up to 2 years, so we can show how we handled them.
  • Payment and invoice records: 6 years after the end of the tax year they relate to, because UK tax law requires it. Stripe keeps its own records for the same reason.
  • Server logs: up to 30 days.
  • Accounts unused for 2 years: we'll email you, then delete the account if you don't reply within 30 days.

Your rights

You can ask us to give you a copy of your data, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to how we use it, or give it to you in a portable format. Coaches can download all their data and delete their account themselves under Team week → Your data & help. For anything else, email us or use the Privacy request form. We'll reply within one month.

Complaints

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your data, please tell us first: email {{contactEmail}} or use the Privacy request form. We'll acknowledge your complaint within 30 days, look into it, keep you updated and tell you the outcome without undue delay.

You can also complain to the Information Commission at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint or on 0303 123 1113.

Security

All connections are encrypted (HTTPS). Passwords are hashed with scrypt and never stored in readable form. Login cookies can't be read by scripts. Our server is firewalled, kept up to date and backed up nightly. If a breach ever puts your data at risk, we'll tell you and the Information Commission as the law requires.

Cookies

We use one essential cookie to keep coaches logged in, and nothing for tracking or adverts. See our Cookies page.

Changes

If we make a significant change, we'll tell coaches by email or in the app at least 14 days before it takes effect, and ask you to agree again where needed.
