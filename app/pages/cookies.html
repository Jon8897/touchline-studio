Paying with Stripe

When you subscribe, you're taken to Stripe's secure checkout page (on stripe.com). Stripe sets its own strictly necessary cookies there to process the payment and prevent fraud. See Stripe's cookie policy.

Storage on your device

The app also saves some things in your browser's local storage so it works quickly and offline: your last-viewed screen, completed fitness sessions, demo-mode work and a copy of your plans. This stays on your device. You can clear it in your browser settings, or by logging out and clearing site data.

What we don't use

No analytics, advertising, social media or third-party cookies. Fonts and code are served from our own server, so no other company sees your visit. If we ever add privacy-friendly statistics, we'll update this page first and, if they need consent, ask you.

Strictly necessary cookies

Under the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations, cookies that are strictly necessary for a service you've asked for, like staying logged in, don't need consent. You can block cookies in your browser, but you won't be able to log in.
